# Starting Clamp
3D printable starting clamp CAD

### About
This project is intended to be a 3D printable clamp to help with stringing tennis rackets that can provide all the functionality a starting clamp is used for 

I decided to make this because I had success using 3d printed flying clamps to string a tennis racket. Specifically, [this](https://www.thingiverse.com/thing:4597862) design. However, I couldn't find any 3d printable designs for a starting clamp, which is a very useful tool that makes it easier to start the mains, tie knots, and pull tension when you're running low on string. But these 3d printed clamps proved to me that a 3d printed design capable of holding 50+ lbs of tension was possible, so I decided to CAD a starting clamp myself.

Basically all starting clamps you can buy have the same basic design with spring loaded handles. My design is pretty different because It would be practically impossible to 3d print springs that can provide enough clamping force. Also, I think that the overall design is just generally flawed and would be especially bad if it were made with plastic.

This design uses a tensioning lever and adjustable knob that passes through two clamping plates and uses the natural flex of plastic to clamp a string with an adjustable level of force. This is how the flying clamps in the link above work. It could be a bit less convinent to have to latch and unlatch the lever whenever you want to clamp, but it should also be able to be less harsh on softer strings

### Assembly
I haven't built this yet myself, but here's you would go about it

- Use petg plastic. its strong and also has quite a bit of flex which is how the tension is actually applied. pretty cheap and works on almost all printers
- In the slicer, I would do about 40% gyroid infill and like 8 or more walls. the walls are the part that really matters. Standard 0.2 mm layer height should be fine.
- print each of the models provided in the stl folder. you'll want to print two of the clamp plates, both sides are identical.
- assembly is pretty simple. cut out pieces of ~150 grit and paper to and glue into the slots on the clamping plates. align them toward each other and put the threaded dowel through the hold in the middle. slide the spacer over one side and screw the knob on top. take the lever and use a nail or screw to secure it in the hole on the other side of the dowel. Fit the spring into the slots at the top of the open jaws. Pull the lever down and tighten the knob until the desired clamping force is achieved on the string. The hinge at the bottom is designed to use a piece of filament through the center, so cut a piece to length and then use whatever you want to hold it in place
